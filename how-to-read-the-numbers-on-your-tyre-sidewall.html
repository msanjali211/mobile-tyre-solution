How to Read the Numbers on Your Tyre Sidewall

Every tyre carries a code on its sidewall that tells you its size, load rating and speed rating. It looks cryptic, but it is simple once you know the pattern. Take 205/55 R16 91V, a common size:

205 is the width in millimetres. 55 is the profile — the sidewall height as a percentage of the width. R16 means radial construction, 16-inch wheel. 91 is the load index — how much weight each tyre can carry. V is the speed rating — the maximum speed the tyre is rated for.

Fitting the wrong size or a lower load or speed rating than the manufacturer specifies is not just a bad idea, it can affect your insurance and your MOT. Always match what the car maker recommends, which is printed on the driver's door jamb or in the handbook.
